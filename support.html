Our words don't match

  1. Open Settings (the small "S" at lower left) and compare the Seed check code on each phone. If the codes differ, someone typed a different seed. Spelling, capital letters and spaces inside the seed all matter. Re-enter it on the phone that differs.
  2. Compare Words change every. Everyone must use the same frequency.
  3. Check the UTC time shown at the bottom of the main screen. Phones must set the time automatically: on iPhone, Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically; on Android, Settings → System → Date & time → Set time automatically.
  4. Make sure everyone has the same app version.

I forgot the seed

The seed is write-only and cannot be shown again, by design. Ask whoever created the group for it and enter it again.

The widget is out of date

Widgets are refreshed by the phone's system. Open the app once to refresh it. On Android, allowing Alarms & reminders for SafetyKey lets the widget change on the exact minute. Adding the widget again also forces a refresh.

Managed (company) devices

If your organisation set the seed or frequency, those settings are locked and show "managed by your organisation". Contact your IT administrator.
