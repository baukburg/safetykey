SafetyKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

SafetyKey does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It works entirely on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Group seed (if you enter one): stored securely on your device (in the iOS Keychain on iPhone, or encrypted with an Android Keystore key on Android). It is write-only: the app never displays it after saving. It is excluded from iCloud and Google backups and device-to-device transfers, and it is never sent anywhere.
  • Preferences: your chosen background colour and word-change frequency, stored locally.
  • Widget data: derived values needed to draw the widget, shared only between the app and its widget on your device. The seed itself is not shared with the widget.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network connections and has no servers or accounts.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • It has no internet permission on Android.

Managed devices

If your organisation deploys SafetyKey through mobile device management, your administrator can supply a seed and word-change frequency to the app through managed app configuration (Apple managed app configuration on iPhone, Android managed configurations on Android). That configuration is controlled by your organisation, not by the app developer.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
